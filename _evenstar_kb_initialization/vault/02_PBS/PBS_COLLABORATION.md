# PBS collaboration

Future collaboration may include users, organizations, teams, project permissions, presence, comments, mentions, notifications, locking where needed and review of proposed changes.

The source does not choose locking versus conflict resolution, offline reconciliation or realtime transport. Collaboration must preserve project authorization and traceable engineering changes. Specify these mechanisms against revision semantics before declaring a concurrent editing design.
