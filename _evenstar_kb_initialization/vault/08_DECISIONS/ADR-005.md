# ADR-005 — Explicit authoritative data ownership

## Context

Shared information may be stored/displayed in several systems.

## Decision

Each datum has one explicit authoritative owner. Others may reference, display, cache or propose changes through a controlled boundary.

## Consequences

Ownership is field/domain-based, not determined simply by storage product. Ambiguous fields remain questions; no subsystem silently writes another owner.

## Alternatives

Uncontrolled duplicated authorities or automatic bidirectional overwrite would compromise the plant and calculation basis.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
