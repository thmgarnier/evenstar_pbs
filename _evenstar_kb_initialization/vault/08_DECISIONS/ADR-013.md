# ADR-013 — Selected PSS evidence enters PMS

## Context

The calculation domain has more runs/results than controlled delivery needs.

## Decision

Normally only selected/promoted PSS engineering evidence crosses into PMS; PSS remains calculation source while PMS owns delivery/promotion context.

## Consequences

Evidence needs stable identity and relation to a deliverable revision. Publication into the PSS register is a separate operation.

## Alternatives

Dumping every PSS result into PMS would duplicate the computation domain and obscure the delivery basis.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
