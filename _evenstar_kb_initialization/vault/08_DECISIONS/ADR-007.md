# ADR-007 — Separate PSS and PMS Airtable bases

## Context

Engineering analysis and controlled delivery have different data, volume and workflow responsibilities.

## Decision

Keep the PSS Engineering Register and PMS project delivery base separate, crossing the boundary through selected references/evidence.

## Consequences

PSS publication is not PMS issue control. Shared identity ties the bases together; separate does not mean disconnected.

## Alternatives

One merged engineering/delivery base conflicts with the explicit source guardrail.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
