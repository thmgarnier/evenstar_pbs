# ADR-009 — No silent PSS modification of PBS state

## Context

Study alternatives must not corrupt the physical design basis.

## Decision

PSS owns its model/study overrides and must not silently overwrite canonical PBS plant state.

## Consequences

Imported PBS values remain source-linked; a candidate design change uses a separate proposal.

## Alternatives

Treating a simulation diameter override as an immediate plant update would cross authority without review.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
