# ADR-003 — KKS and tags are human identifiers

## Context

Engineering tags matter to users but can change under revision.

## Decision

KKS/tags are first-class attributes and human identifiers, not relational primary identity.

## Consequences

Controlled retagging preserves relationships. Tag grammar and uniqueness rules remain open.

## Alternatives

Use KKS as relational key; renaming then risks breaking references.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
