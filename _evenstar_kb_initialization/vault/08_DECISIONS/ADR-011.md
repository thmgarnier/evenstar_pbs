# ADR-011 — Explicit PSS design-change proposals

## Context

An engineering study may justify a better physical design.

## Decision

PSS-to-PBS changes are explicit proposals with evidence; PBS controls acceptance/application or rejection.

## Consequences

Applied changes create traceable plant revisions. The precise PMS review coordination and transaction remain unresolved.

## Alternatives

Silently promoting a local study or using PMS status alone as canonical mutation authority is not accepted.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
