# ADR-010 — Reuse PBS topology for PSS hydraulics

## Context

PBS already knows physical networks that traditional simulations would ask engineers to redraw.

## Decision

PSS consumes sufficient PBS physical topology/data to construct hydraulic models without re-entry of known network information.

## Consequences

PSS adds missing simulation assumptions and preserves source versions. The exact extraction/completeness contract still needs design.

## Alternatives

Manually recreating a known network wastes information and risks divergence.

Alternatives here are curator analysis, not a claim that a historical options review occurred.
