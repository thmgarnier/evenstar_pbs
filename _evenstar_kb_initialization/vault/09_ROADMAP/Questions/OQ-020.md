# OQ-020 — PSS publication selection policy

**OPEN QUESTION — P1**

## Decision needed

Decide which engineering-resolution results are published automatically and which require explicit selection.

## Options / investigation

Preserve curated resolution either way. This question concerns the PSS register; PMS still receives selected/promoted evidence only.

## Why it matters

Avoids misreading a register publication as formal delivery approval.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.
