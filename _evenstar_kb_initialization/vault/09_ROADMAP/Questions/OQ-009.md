# OQ-009 — Units, basis and chemical identities

**OPEN QUESTION — P1**

## Decision needed

Define unit normalization, composition bases and mappings to canonical components/species.

## Options / investigation

Inventory source units and distinguish mole/mass/concentration bases; choose chemical identity mapping with explicit unknowns.

## Why it matters

Prevents apparently reusable data from changing scientific meaning.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
