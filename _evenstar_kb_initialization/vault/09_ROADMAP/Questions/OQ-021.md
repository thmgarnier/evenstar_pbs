# OQ-021 — Field-level cross-domain ownership

**OPEN QUESTION — P0**

## Decision needed

Assign authority to overlapping project metadata, equipment/vendor parameters, design basis and reference/cache fields.

## Options / investigation

Separate PBS physical/design values from PSS reusable computational assumptions and PMS delivery metadata. Record owner, allowed writes, unit/basis and version for each field.

## Why it matters

A broad owner table cannot silently settle ambiguous individual fields.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
