# OQ-002 — KKS representation and change rules

**OPEN QUESTION — P1**

## Decision needed

Specify KKS/tag structure, namespaces, validation and revision rules without using tags as relational primary identity.

## Options / investigation

The corpus gives examples, not the applicable normative KKS grammar. Obtain the intended coding convention before enforcing it.

## Why it matters

Affects tags, search, visibility and cached references.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
