# OQ-013 — Local/offline, collaboration and desktop

**OPEN QUESTION — P1**

## Decision needed

Define local versus cloud state, offline project-data caching, conflict/locking model, undo/redo persistence and later desktop/hybrid direction.

## Options / investigation

Separate basic local calculation from collaboration needs; decide cache freshness and reconnect behavior against revision rules.

## Why it matters

Avoids choosing infrastructure before data authority/concurrency semantics.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
