# OQ-004 — Geometry, synchronization and smart routing

**OPEN QUESTION — P1**

## Decision needed

Choose geometry representation, P&ID/3D update semantics, routing constraints/objectives, supports/racks and editing/undo behavior.

## Options / investigation

Start from editable physical topology/geometry. Then compare routing candidates against obstacles, clearances, manual waypoints and locked segments. No algorithm is selected.

## Why it matters

Prevents renderer state or an automatic route from silently becoming a separate plant definition.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
