# OQ-006 — Hydraulic extraction contract

**OPEN QUESTION — P0**

## Decision needed

Specify sufficient PBS topology/geometry/properties, stable node/edge mapping, missing-data handling and source-version semantics.

## Options / investigation

Work a compact network including a branch, elevation, valve/fitting and pump. Distinguish nominal/inside diameter and known physical data from PSS assumptions.

## Why it matters

Required for no-redraw hydraulic reuse and meaningful ordered node publication.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
