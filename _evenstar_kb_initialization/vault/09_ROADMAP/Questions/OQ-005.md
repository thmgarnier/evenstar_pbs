# OQ-005 — Plant revisions and PBS/PMS change review

**OPEN QUESTION — P0**

## Decision needed

Define plant change sets, review roles and the transaction between PSS proposal, PMS coordination and PBS acceptance/application.

## Options / investigation

Separate review approval from canonical application; define stale baseline, rejection, before/after, evidence and revision links. The exact state machine remains open.

## Why it matters

Preserves authority and traceability across engineering and delivery.

## Resolution path

Prepare a concrete domain example and options, identify affected owners/contracts/requirements, and obtain an explicit architectural resolution. Record it in an ADR or requirement update; preserve this question with its resolution link.

No individual owner or deadline has been assigned.
