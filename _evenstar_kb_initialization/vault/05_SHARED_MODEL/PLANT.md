# Plant

A Plant is a plant-definition context within a Project, organizing Systems and engineering objects. PBS owns its hierarchy and physical definition. A project may need more than one plant context, but exact cardinality is not ratified.

PSS models and PMS delivery groupings reference plant/system context without taking ownership of it.
