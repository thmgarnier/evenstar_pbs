# Document

A document has identity and metadata distinct from its binary file, deliverable and revisions. PMS owns document-control metadata and issued delivery context. PBS links applicable documents to plant objects. PSS produces calculation reports/evidence.

Exact Deliverable ↔ Document ↔ Revision cardinalities, binary storage authority, retention and access are unresolved. Do not silently nominate either PBS storage or Airtable attachments as the sole controlled-file system.
