# Equipment

Equipment is a typed canonical engineering object such as a pump, valve, vessel or exchanger. PBS owns its existence, identity, placement and physical/design facets. Ports/nozzles connect it to the plant.

PSS may own calculation configuration, operating assumptions and performance results associated with it. Exact ownership of vendor/equipment parameter fields must be mapped explicitly.
