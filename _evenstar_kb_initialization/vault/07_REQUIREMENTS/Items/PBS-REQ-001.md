# PBS-REQ-001 — Plant hierarchy and typed relationships

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS shall organize Project/Plant/System engineering objects and expose first-class typed relationships.

## Acceptance criteria

Navigate from a project to system equipment, its ports and connected objects without reconstructing relationships from drawings.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
