# PBS-REQ-004 — Engineering 3D interaction

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS 3D shall render/select canonical objects and support equipment placement, movement, rotation and physical connection.

## Acceptance criteria

Move/select equipment and verify its canonical identity/placement and connected engineering context.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
