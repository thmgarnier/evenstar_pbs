# PBS-REQ-005 — Metadata-driven visibility

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS shall support KKS/system-based visibility derived from canonical metadata.

## Acceptance criteria

Hide/show a system or KKS selection and verify visibility follows plant metadata rather than an unrelated renderer-only list.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
