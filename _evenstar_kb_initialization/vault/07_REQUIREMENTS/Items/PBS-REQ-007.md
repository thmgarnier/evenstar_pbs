# PBS-REQ-007 — Editable generated routes

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

Generated PBS pipe routes shall remain inspectable and editable by engineers.

## Acceptance criteria

Modify a generated route and inspect resulting geometry and engineering data; unresolved routing constraints are visible.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
