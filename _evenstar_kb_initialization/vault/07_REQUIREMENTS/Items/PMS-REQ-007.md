# PMS-REQ-007 — Issued revision preservation

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall supersede issued revisions with new revisions rather than silently overwrite issued history.

## Acceptance criteria

Change issued content through a new revision; both the earlier issued state and supersession remain traceable.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
