# PBS-REQ-006 — Piping engineering definition

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS shall represent physical piping with endpoints, topology, physical properties, geometry and revision.

## Acceptance criteria

Inspect a route and retrieve linked UUIDs, ports, diameter/specification, geometry/elevation and revision for hydraulic reuse.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
