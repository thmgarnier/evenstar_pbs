# PSS-REQ-005 — Reusable composition and case data

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PSS Project Data shall support reusable compositions, engineering variables and operating/design cases.

## Acceptance criteria

Reuse one versioned composition in two inputs and resolve its components/species, basis, source and case without repeated unrelated columns.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
