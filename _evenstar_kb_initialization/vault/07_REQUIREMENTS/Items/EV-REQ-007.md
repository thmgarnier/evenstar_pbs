# EV-REQ-007 — Human engineering authority

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

AI assistance shall not automatically approve engineering work or silently change authoritative controlled state.

## Acceptance criteria

An AI suggestion remains a suggestion until an authorized explicit decision; document generation enters normal review.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
