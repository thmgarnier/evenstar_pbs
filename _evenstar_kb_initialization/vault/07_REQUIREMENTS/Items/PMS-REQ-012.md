# PMS-REQ-012 — Evidence supersession awareness

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Proposed product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS should surface when selected PSS evidence is superseded without silently replacing issued support.

## Acceptance criteria

A newer selected-evidence version is flagged for review while the issued revision retains its original link.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
