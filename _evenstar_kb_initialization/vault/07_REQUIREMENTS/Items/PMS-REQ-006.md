# PMS-REQ-006 — Review and approval history

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall preserve actor, exact revision, time, decision/status and review comments/responses.

## Acceptance criteria

Retrieve an earlier review/approval with its revision after a later revision is created.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
