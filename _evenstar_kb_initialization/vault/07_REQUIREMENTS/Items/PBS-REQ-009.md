# PBS-REQ-009 — Controlled plant revisions

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS shall record proposed engineering changes with affected objects, reason, origin, before/after and traceable acceptance/application or rejection.

## Acceptance criteria

A rejected proposal leaves plant state unchanged; an applied accepted proposal links to a new revision and origin evidence.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
