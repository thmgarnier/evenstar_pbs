# PMS-REQ-008 — Configurable delivery lifecycle

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall offer a strong default delivery workflow and permit project/client-specific configuration.

## Acceptance criteria

An agreed default and one configured variation preserve explicit review/approval/issue semantics and revision history.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
