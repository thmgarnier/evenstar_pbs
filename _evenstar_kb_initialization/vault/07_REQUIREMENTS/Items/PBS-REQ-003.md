# PBS-REQ-003 — P&ID validation and navigation

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS P&ID shall expose tags, completeness/validation, revision awareness and navigation to related representations.

## Acceptance criteria

Inspect a tagged object, a missing/invalid connection and its revision; navigate to its data/3D context.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
