# EV-REQ-006 — Traceable controlled change

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

Engineering and issued delivery changes shall preserve traceable history.

## Acceptance criteria

Retrieve the prior revision, origin/reason and successor rather than observing an unexplained overwrite.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
