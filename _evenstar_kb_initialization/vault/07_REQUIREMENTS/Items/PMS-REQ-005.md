# PMS-REQ-005 — Revision-specific evidence

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall identify the engineering evidence supporting a deliverable/document revision.

## Acceptance criteria

Open an issued revision and resolve its PBS references, selected PSS evidence and review/approval context.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
