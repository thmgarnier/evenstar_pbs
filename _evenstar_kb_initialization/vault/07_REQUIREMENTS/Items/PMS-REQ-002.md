# PMS-REQ-002 — Multi-discipline project model

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall support multiple engineering disciplines and project phases without requiring their technical databases inside PMS.

## Acceptance criteria

Represent deliverables for at least three disciplines in an MVP fixture, referencing external engineering where applicable.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
