# PMS-REQ-001 — Deliverable-centered delivery

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PMS shall center project work on deliverables with supporting tasks, milestones, responsibility, dates and status.

## Acceptance criteria

Navigate from a deliverable to tasks and delivery context; task completion alone does not imply issue approval.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
