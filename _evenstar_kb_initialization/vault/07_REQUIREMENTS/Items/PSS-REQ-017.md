# PSS-REQ-017 — Dynamics and custom block contracts

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Proposed product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

Future dynamic/control studies should define typed signals and coordinated execution without automatic high-frequency Airtable publication.

## Acceptance criteria

An agreed controller/block scenario records its execution assumptions and publishes only selected engineering summaries.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
