# EV-REQ-009 — Domain before technology

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

Implementation planning shall derive technology choices from engineering requirements, domain responsibilities and contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

A proposed technology change cites the behavior/model/contract it serves and retains their semantics.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
