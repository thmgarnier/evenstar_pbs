# PSS-REQ-010 — Engineering-resolution publication

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PSS shall publish curated meaningful engineering results while retaining detailed computation in its domain.

## Acceptance criteria

A hydraulic result publication includes selected stations/run/pipeline/node identity, order/distance/elevation, units and case without every solver cell.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
