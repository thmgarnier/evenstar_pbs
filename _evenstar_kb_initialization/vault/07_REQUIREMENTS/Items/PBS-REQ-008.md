# PBS-REQ-008 — Engineering-data explorer

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

PBS shall browse/search equipment, instruments, materials, parts, specifications, hierarchy, tags, relationships and documents.

## Acceptance criteria

Find a known object by UUID/tag/name/system and inspect connected information from canonical records.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
