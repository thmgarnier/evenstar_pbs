# PSS-REQ-011 — Analytical project register

**Classification:** REQUIREMENT

**Status:** Accepted product intent. **Implementation:** Unverified.

The PSS register shall support engineering comparison and navigation around curated results.

## Acceptance criteria

Compare two runs/cases, inspect selected chart/KPI data with assumptions/warnings, and navigate to originating models/reports.

These criteria are derived verification targets, not executed product tests.
