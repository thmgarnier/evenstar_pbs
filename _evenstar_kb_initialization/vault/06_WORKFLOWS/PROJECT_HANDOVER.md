# Project handover

**Trigger:** A project approaches controlled completion/handover.

## Flow

1. Identify required deliverables, issued revisions, approvals and outstanding punch items.
2. Preserve links to canonical plant context and selected engineering evidence.
3. Prepare a controlled handover/archive view with project and template/version context.

## Ownership and exceptions

This is a proposed future workflow outline. Handover scope, as-built reconciliation, retention/export and acceptance responsibility are not specified by the corpus.

## Intended completion evidence

Future acceptance criteria must be agreed before this can be an implementation-ready handover workflow.
