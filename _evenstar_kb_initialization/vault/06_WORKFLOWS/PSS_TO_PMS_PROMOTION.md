# PSS to PMS evidence promotion

**Trigger:** Engineer selects calculation evidence for a controlled deliverable.

## Flow

1. Select a PSS model/run/report/study or design-basis outcome.
2. Associate its stable identity and provenance with the target PMS project/deliverable revision.
3. Preserve PSS as the calculation source and PMS as owner of promotion/delivery context.
4. Review, approve and issue through normal document control.
5. Surface superseded evidence for review; do not silently replace issued-revision support.

## Ownership and exceptions

Promotion is selective and distinct from publication into the PSS register. Payload, pinning and supersession detection are unresolved.

## Intended completion evidence

The deliverable revision answers exactly what engineering evidence supports it without copying all PSS runs.
